## Who Plays Clark, Mary, and Phil? A Closer Look at the Leads

The top of the **Backrooms cast** list carries most of the film's emotional weight. Here is what we know about each performer and the role they are credited with.

Ejiofor is an Academy Award nominee with a filmography that runs from *12 Years a Slave* to *Doctor Strange* and *The Man Who Fell to Earth*. Placing an actor of his gravity at the top of the call sheet signals that Clark is the film's anchor — most likely the adult protagonist whose perspective we follow into the maze.

### Renate Reinsve as Mary

Reinsve broke out internationally with *The Worst Person in the World* and has since become one of the most in-demand European actors of her generation. Her casting as Mary, paired with Ember Ambrose as Young Mary, suggests a character with a past that the film actually explores rather than just references.

### Mark Duplass as Phil

Duplass brings a very specific energy: warm, ordinary, and slightly unsettling when the script asks for it, as anyone who saw *Creep* knows. He is also credited with a wife and two children in the cast list, which makes Phil the most conventionally "grounded" character in the ensemble — often the person horror films use to measure how far reality has slipped.

### Finn Bennett and Lukita Maxwell

Finn Bennett, who drew attention for *True Detective: Night Country*, plays Bobby. Lukita Maxwell, known for the Apple TV+ series *Shrinking*, plays Kat. Both are younger performers, which fits a common structure in liminal horror: a group that splits up, with the youngest members pushed furthest from safety.
