## Who Is Backrooms Mary in the Kane Pixels Universe?

Backrooms Mary is one of the few named human characters that fans keep returning to in the Kane Pixels Backrooms series, and she never has to outrun a monster to earn that attention. Instead of sprinting through endless yellow corridors, Backrooms Mary — catalogued on the community wiki as Dr. Mary Kline — spends her screen time on a quieter and far more unsettling question: what happens to the people left behind when someone disappears into a place that should not exist?

If you have fallen down the Kane Pixels rabbit hole, you have probably seen her name in comment sections, wiki pages, and theory threads, usually attached to the same handful of debates. This guide gathers what the community has documented about her, what her visual design is quietly telling you, and why she matters to the wider Backrooms mythos.

Kane Pixels — the found-footage project that went viral in 2022 and turned a single creepypasta image into a full narrative universe — builds its horror out of ordinary, bureaucratic people. Mary Kline is one of them.

The community wiki describes her as a slender white woman with shoulder-length dark brown hair and brown eyes. That is a deliberately plain description, and that is exactly the point: she is not an entity, not a soldier, and not a survivor of some dramatic fall through reality. She is a person with a job, a wardrobe, and a case file.

## Mary Kline's Wardrobe Is Doing the Storytelling
