### What the Missing Posters Actually Reveal

The most unsettling detail in Kat's documented story is not that she vanished. It is that the search for her was, in effect, theater. That single narrative choice does more worldbuilding than any chase sequence. It tells you that whatever organization operates behind the found-footage material has both the reach to lose a person and the confidence to manage the story afterward. For a series built on grainy camcorder footage, that is a remarkably bureaucratic kind of horror.
